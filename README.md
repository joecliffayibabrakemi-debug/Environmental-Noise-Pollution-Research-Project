# Assessment of Environmental Noise Pollution in Selected Markets in Yenagoa and Its Environs, Nigeria

## About the Project

This project is my undergraduate research project carried out as part of my **B.Sc. Physics** programme at Federal University Otuoke, Bayelsa State, Nigeria.

The study assessed environmental noise levels in selected markets in Yenagoa and its environs and examined the level of noise pollution recorded at the different locations.

Five market locations were considered:

* Mbiama Market
* Opolo Market
* Swali Market — Foodstuff Section
* Swali Market — Electrical Parts
* Otuoke Market

The research involved field measurements using a **TES-1350A sound level meter** and GPS, followed by computational analysis of the recorded results.

---

## Research Objectives

The study was carried out to:

* Measure the equivalent noise levels at the selected markets.
* Assess the level of noise pollution resulting from market activities.
* Identify possible sources of noise within the selected environments.
* Examine the possible effects of environmental noise on health and quality of life.

---

## Methodology

The research combined **experimental measurements and computational analysis**.

A TES-1350A sound level meter was used to obtain noise measurements, while GPS was used to obtain location information for the selected sites.

The Noise Pollution Level (Lnp) was calculated using:

```text
Lnp = Leq + Kσ
```

where:

* `Lnp` = Noise Pollution Level
* `Leq` = Equivalent Sound Level
* `K` = 2.56
* `σ` = Standard deviation of the acquired Leq values

## The study also used **Golden Software Surfer V28.1.248** for map analysis.

## Results

The reported results for the five locations were:

| Market                          | Leq (dBA) | Lnp (dB) |
| ------------------------------- | --------: | -------: |
| Mbiama Market                   |     75.50 |    89.02 |
| Opolo Market                    |     67.80 |    81.32 |
| Swali Market — Foodstuff        |     65.10 |    78.62 |
| Swali Market — Electrical Parts |     70.90 |    84.42 |
| Otuoke Market                   |     61.80 |    75.62 |

Mbiama Market recorded the highest Noise Pollution Level at **89.02 dB**, while Otuoke Market recorded the lowest at **75.62 dB**.

The research reported that measurements were taken between **11:00 AM and 3:00 PM** and that the Lnp values across the selected markets ranged from **75.62 dB to 89.02 dB**.

---

## Findings

The study found that the noise levels recorded at the selected markets were above the reference level discussed in the research.

Possible contributors identified in the study included:

* Traffic
* Market activities
* Crowding
* Proximity to roads

The study also discussed the possible effects of prolonged exposure to elevated noise levels.

---

## Recommendations

The research recommended measures including:

* Regular maintenance of equipment.
* Use of noise barriers where appropriate.
* Use of quieter equipment.
* Increased awareness of noise pollution.
* Use of hearing protection where necessary.
* Health checks for people exposed to elevated noise levels.
